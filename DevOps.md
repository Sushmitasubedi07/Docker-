
# **What is DevOps?**

DevOps combines **development (Dev)** and **operations (Ops)** to increase the efficiency, speed, and security of software development and delivery compared to traditional processes.

---

## **Who is a DevOps Engineer?**

A **DevOps engineer** is someone who bridges the gap between software development and IT operations. They ensure faster, more efficient software delivery by implementing **automation, CI/CD pipelines, cloud infrastructure, and monitoring tools**. Their role is critical in maintaining a seamless workflow from development to production.

---

## **When Did DevOps Become a Thing?**

In **2009**, at the **O’Reilly Velocity Conference**, **John Allspaw** (Senior Vice-President of Technical Operations) and **Paul Hammond** (Director of Engineering) gave a presentation titled, **[“10+ Deployments per Day: Dev and Ops Cooperation at Flickr”](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LdOe18KhtT4)** – and **[the rest is history!](https://everythingdevops.dev/a-brief-history-of-devops-and-its-impact-on-software-development/)**.

Through their talk, they made a case on why **communication and collaboration** between development and operations are vital, which sparked the appearance of what we now know as **DevOps**. **Patrick Debois** identified with the situation the two were describing – developers and operation engineers pointing fingers at each other and passing the blame (“it’s not my code, it’s your machines!”). Encouraged by others on social media, he decided to form his own conference, **[DevOps Days](https://www.devopsdays.org/)** in Ghent.

As the demand for **DevOps professionals** grows, companies are actively seeking experts in **automation, [CI/CD](https://www.browserstack.com/guide/top-ci-cd-tools), [cloud computing](https://aws.amazon.com/what-is-cloud-computing/), and [Agile methodologies](https://www.geeksforgeeks.org/what-is-agile-methodology/)**. ## **The DevOps Roadmap**

Here’s a structured roadmap to help you navigate the learning journey:

### 1. **Fundamentals of Linux and Networking**
   - Learn Linux commands and shell scripting.
   - Understand networking concepts (DNS, Load Balancing, Proxy, Firewalls).
   - Work with SSH, FTP, and VPN.

### 2. **Version Control with Git**
   - Master Git commands.
   - Learn GitHub or GitLab for collaborative development.

### 3. **Scripting and Programming**
   - Bash scripting for automation.
   - Python for writing infrastructure as code (IaC).

### 4. **[CI/CD Pipelines](https://www.browserstack.com/guide/top-ci-cd-tools)**
   - Understand Continuous Integration and Continuous Deployment.
   - Tools: **[Jenkins](https://www.jenkins.io/)**, **[GitHub Actions](https://github.com/features/actions)**, **[GitLab CI/CD](https://docs.gitlab.com/ee/ci/)**, **[CircleCI](https://circleci.com/)**.

### 5. **Infrastructure as Code (IaC)**
   - Learn **[Terraform](https://www.terraform.io/)** and **[Ansible](https://www.ansible.com/)**.
   - Automate cloud infrastructure deployment.

### 6. **Containerization and Orchestration**
   - **[Docker](https://www.docker.com/)**: Learn how to containerize applications.
   - **[Kubernetes](https://kubernetes.io/)**: Deploy and manage containers at scale.

### 7. **[Cloud Computing](https://aws.amazon.com/what-is-cloud-computing/)**
   - **[AWS](https://aws.amazon.com/)**, **[Azure](https://azure.microsoft.com/)**, or **[Google Cloud Platform (GCP)](https://cloud.google.com/)**.
   - Work with EC2, S3, IAM, Lambda, and serverless computing.

### 8. **Monitoring and Logging**
   - **[Prometheus](https://prometheus.io/)**, **[Grafana](https://grafana.com/)**, **[ELK Stack](https://www.elastic.co/what-is/elk-stack)** (Elasticsearch, Logstash, Kibana).
   - Understand log analysis and application performance monitoring.

### 9. **Security (DevSecOps)**
   - Learn about security best practices in DevOps.
   - Implement security scanning tools: **[SonarQube](https://www.sonarqube.org/)**, **[Trivy](https://github.com/aquasecurity/trivy)**, **[OWASP ZAP](https://owasp.org/www-project-zap/)**.
   - Secrets management with **[HashiCorp Vault](https://www.vaultproject.io/)** and **[AWS Parameter Store](https://aws.amazon.com/systems-manager/features/parameter-store/)**.

---

This roadmap provides a comprehensive guide to mastering DevOps, whether you're in Nepal or anywhere else in the world. Happy learning! 🚀
